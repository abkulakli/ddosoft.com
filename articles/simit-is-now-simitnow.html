SimIt Is Now SimItNow

Our OCPP charge point simulator has a new name and a new home. Here is what changed, why we changed it, and what you need to do about it.

What changed

SimIt is now SimItNow. The product moves off the simit.ddosoft.com subdomain to its own domain, www.simitnow.com. It is its own product now rather than a subdomain of DDOSoft. Nothing about your account, your simulated stations, or your existing test setups needs to change beyond the URL you use to reach it. The rename touches only the product's name and address. The simulator's behaviour, the OCPP versions it supports, and the data in your account all stay as they are, so there is nothing in your existing setups to reconfigure.

Why the rename

The rename was necessary to resolve a naming conflict with an existing trademark. We would rather deal with that early, while the product is still growing its user base, than build more brand recognition around a name we might later be forced to change under worse circumstances. The product's direction has not changed, and this was not a rebranding exercise for its own sake. It was the direct fix for a specific naming problem.

What SimItNow does

For readers new to the product: SimItNow is a charge point simulator. It speaks both OCPP 1.6 and OCPP 2.0.1, and it stands in for physical EV charging stations so that the team running a central system, a CSMS, can test against realistic charger behaviour without buying hardware. You can run many simulated stations at once against a real backend, which makes it useful for load testing and for exercising the edge cases that are awkward to reproduce with physical equipment: reconnects, offline authorisation, charging-profile handling. A free plan is available for smaller test setups.

What stays the same

The simulator itself is unchanged by the rename: the OCPP 1.6 and 2.0.1 support, the ability to run many simulated stations against real backends, and the free plan all carry over exactly as they were. The team behind it hasn't changed either, and there is no migration for existing users to perform beyond updating the address they use to reach the product.

What to update if you link to it

If you have bookmarks, documentation, or code samples pointing at simit.ddosoft.com, update them to www.simitnow.com. The old subdomain is still live for now, so nothing breaks immediately, but new integrations and links should point at the new domain going forward, and we'd encourage updating existing references when it's convenient rather than waiting. If you have automated tests pointing at the old address, update those too.

Questions about the rename, or about integrating OCPP 1.6 or 2.0.1 with SimItNow, get in touch.

The new domain is live as of today. We'll keep this page as the reference for the rename. If anything about the transition changes, such as the eventual retirement of the old subdomain, we'll update it here rather than opening a separate announcement.
